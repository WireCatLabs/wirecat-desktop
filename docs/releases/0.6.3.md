WireCat 0.6.3 adds the Asterisk phone tool, shows calls on CRM cards and lets you see an Email account's mail rules. A problem report on a slow network now has time to send.

## New

- **Asterisk.** WireCat can now install the Asterisk tool. It reads the calls and recordings of your phone system and keeps each call as a meeting. When you sign in, you choose where the calls are: a Master.csv file, MySQL or PostgreSQL. A database password goes to the tool directly and is not saved in a setting.
- **Calls on CRM cards.** A Bitrix24 deal or lead card lists the calls and recorded meetings of the people on it. A person card shows their latest messages, mail and up to ten calls. Open a call to see its details and, when there is one, its transcript.
- **Mail rules.** An Email account's menu has "Mail rules". It lists the account's rules: what each one matches, what it does, its tags and who made it. The list is read only. Above it is the command to add a rule.
- **Meeting transcripts from Google Drive.** Google Meet saves transcripts and Gemini notes in the organizer's Drive, often outside the folders you picked. After you sign in to Drive, and in the account's folder settings, WireCat now asks whether to add them. This needs a Drive tool that can list transcripts: WireCat asks you to update it first.
- **Unread count on the Ubuntu dock.** The dock icon shows how many notices the bell has unread.

## Improved

- **Bitrix24 sign-in.** The rights step names the section in Bitrix24 where you set them and shows a picture of it. The portal field asks for the domain only. When Bitrix24 refuses for missing rights, the hint names all three rights it needs.
- **Telegram sign-in.** A warning before the app form on my.telegram.org says that one refused try can lock the page for hours.
- **Same look on every page.** Errors, buttons, fields and headers look and behave the same on every page. The Back button sits next to Cancel and Next in every step-by-step window. A focused text field has one clear border.

## Fixed

- **A problem report on a slow network.** A report with its logs had 30 seconds to connect, upload and get an answer. On a slow or VPN connection it could fail with "Could not reach the WireCat server". Now it has 2 minutes to send. If the server cannot be reached at all, the error still comes within 10 seconds.
- **Problem reports about newer tools.** The WireCat server now accepts reports about Bitrix24, Asterisk and any newer tool. This was fixed on the server, so it works in every version of WireCat.
- **A chat's profiles.** The conversation header shows every profile that has the chat, not only the first one.
- **Installing and signing in at once.** Installing a tool and signing in to it at the same time no longer block each other.
- **Copy buttons.** A copy button says when the copy fails.
- **Russian counts.** Numbers in Russian text use the right word forms.
- **Reduced motion.** WireCat follows your system's reduced motion setting everywhere.
