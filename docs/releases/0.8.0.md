WireCat 0.8.0 gets new official tools from wirecat.dev without an app update, and says plainly what agents may do at "Ask first". It also brings together what came in 0.7.1 to 0.7.5: new tools, an Inbox for your mail, updates through apt on Linux, and tools you add yourself that sign in inside the window.

## New

- **Official tools from wirecat.dev.** A new official WireCat tool shows up on the Tools page with its icon and texts in your language, without a new version of the app. The app checks the list's signature first. With no network it uses the list built into the app.
- **New tools in the catalog**: amoCRM, Yandex Disk, Attio, HubSpot, Linear and WordPress. Each one signs in inside the window, with steps for the token or password it needs.
- **Inbox.** A tab in Digests where the AI model sorts your mail. Each thread shows its sender, and a finished sort names the model and what it cost. Mail rules open in a dialog with search and pages.
- **Google Calendar syncs from Desktop** and shows when it last did.
- **How fresh WhatsApp data is.** An account says "Live" while its background service is connected, and "Fresh as of 9:05 AM" when it is not.
- **People.** See the last messages, calls, company and deal on each side of a match. See what an agent joined, and undo it.
- **Your own tools sign in inside the window** from their manifest, and can sign out and remove an account.
- **Updates through apt on Linux.** The .deb adds apt.wirecat.dev, so Ubuntu's Software Updater brings new versions.

## Improved

- **"Ask first" says what it means for agents.** Agents connected over MCP make changes at "Ask first" without asking you. The Permissions page now says so under each such setting and when you choose it, in English, Russian and Spanish. To stop them, choose "Read only" or "Block".
- Settings has a tab for the app and a tab for each tool. You can change an account's profile id from the account menu.
- Choose how much mail to sync in Settings.
- The morning CRM digest is in Digests, and you can choose which Notion pages WireCat copies.

## Fixed

- Notion shows its sign-in steps in English, Russian and Spanish, and links to Notion's current token page. A tool without its own steps says what it needs in your language.
- A security update for the cookie library inside the app.

## All changes

- Official tools from the signed list on wirecat.dev, with the built-in list as the fallback (#470)
- Permissions say plainly that agents over MCP make changes at "Ask first" without asking (#471)
- Notion's sign-in steps, and sign-in texts in your language (#466)
- The cookie library updated for a security advisory
