WireCat 0.7.3 lets you sign out of a tool you added yourself, and remove its account.

## New

- **Sign out and remove an account in tools you added yourself.** A tool added by its npm name, such as beeline, can now offer Sign out and Remove account in its account menu. It works once the tool's own new version describes these steps. WireCat checks that they run only the tool's own commands, and asks you before it uses them.
- **Remove an account in the PBX and Notion tools.** Removing an account can now keep or delete its data, once these tools' next versions are installed. Before, you could only sign out.

## Improved

- **Last sync time for more tools.** A tool you added yourself can now tell WireCat when its last sync finished, even when its sync is called something like "meetings sync". beeline gets this with its next version.
- **A shorter screen after sign-in.** The "Connect an AI app" button is gone from it for now. You can still connect an AI app on the tool's page.

## All changes

- An own tool's manifest can sign out and remove accounts, and Connect an AI app is hidden after sign-in (#425)
