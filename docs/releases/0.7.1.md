WireCat 0.7.1 adds amoCRM, puts the morning CRM digest in Digests, lets you choose which Notion pages WireCat copies, and shows what an agent joined in People, with Undo.

## New

- **amoCRM.** amoCRM and Kommo are built-in tools. Sign in with the account's address and a long-lived token, and WireCat copies your deals with their contacts and companies to this computer, so you and your agent can find them and see what changed.
- **The CRM in your digest.** A CRM digest shows its own section: deals won and lost first, then overdue tasks and what moved. Each deal opens in its card.
- **Choose Notion pages.** Notion has the same picker as Google Drive: pick the pages and databases WireCat copies, with Show more for a large workspace. When Notion cuts its search short, the picker says so.
- **What an agent joined.** When an agent merges two entries for one person, People lists the join, and Undo takes it back. A match that waits for you says why.
- **More to decide a match on.** Each side of a match shows its last messages or calls, its company and its deal.

## Improved

- **Logos.** Notion and amoCRM show their own marks, and Notion sits above Bitrix24 and Asterisk on the Tools page.
- **WireCat's own tools.** A tool from WireCat's npm scope, such as beeline, can be added while "Allow tools WireCat has not checked" is off.

## Fixed

- **Deleting Zoom's data deletes it again.** Since zoom-cli 0.74.0 it keeps its data in a new folder, and Delete data now removes that folder.
