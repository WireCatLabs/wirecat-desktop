WireCat 0.6.2 adds a second way to sign in to Bitrix24: through a local application, in the browser. The incoming webhook stays the quick way and works as before.

## New

- **Sign in to Bitrix24 with a local application.** After the portal's address, choose Incoming webhook or Local application. The local application acts with the rights of the employee who signs in, and its sign-in renews by itself. WireCat shows how to make the application in Bitrix24 once, with the address to copy and the three permissions to tick. Then you paste its ID and key, and the portal's sign-in opens in the browser. The key stays on this computer. This needs the Bitrix24 tool 0.14.0 or newer: WireCat asks you to update it first.

## All changes

- Sign in to Bitrix24 through a local application, beside the incoming webhook (#351)
