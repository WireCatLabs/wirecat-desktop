WireCat 0.6.2 adds a second way to sign in to Bitrix24 and lets you remove a Bitrix24 account with its data. In Contacts, your own agent can sort the matches for you, and a match can be one company. «One company» needs Memo 0.131.87 or newer, and Remove account for Bitrix24 needs the Bitrix24 tool 0.15.0 or newer: WireCat offers the update.

## New

- **Sign in to Bitrix24 with a local application.** After the portal's address, choose Incoming webhook or Local application. The local application acts with the rights of the employee who signs in, and its sign-in renews by itself. WireCat shows how to make the application in Bitrix24 once, with the address to copy and the three permissions to tick. Then you paste its ID and key, and the portal's sign-in opens in the browser. The key stays on this computer. This needs the Bitrix24 tool 0.14.0 or newer: WireCat asks you to update it first. The incoming webhook stays the quick way and works as before.
- **Let your agent sort the matches.** The banner above Contacts that says how many matches wait has two new buttons. "Copy the prompt for an agent" copies a prompt for any AI agent. "Run in a terminal" opens your agent with that prompt. The agent looks at each match and its messages, and sorts it into one person, different people, one company or unsure. It cannot decide a match itself, because Memo lets only you decide. At the end it gives you at most three commands to run, and lists the matches it was unsure about, each with a short reason. Each run sorts up to 100 matches. Run it again for the rest.
- **One company.** A match card has "One company" next to "Same person" and "Not the same". Use it when the two contacts are mailboxes of one company, such as sales@ and support@. Their addresses go to the company, the two leave your contacts, and the card says which addresses went to which company. If Memo cannot tell the company, the card asks for its name or for which company it is.
- **Remove a Bitrix24 account with its data.** Remove account for Bitrix24 now asks whether to keep or delete the account's data, lists what goes and what stays, and removes it, as for the other tools.

## Improved

- **A decided match stays in its place.** After you decide a match, its card keeps its place in the list and says what happened. Before, it moved to the end of the list.
- **Clearer check of your own accounts.** When one of the accounts on your own card has another name, for example your bot or a shared mailbox, "Check the links" now says so. It also says what each button does. "These belong together" keeps the account yours, and its messages count as yours in search and digests. "Split" makes it a contact of its own.

## All changes

- Sign in to Bitrix24 through a local application, beside the incoming webhook (#351)
