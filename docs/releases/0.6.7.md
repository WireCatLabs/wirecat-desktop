WireCat 0.6.7 opens a meeting in its calendar from the meeting card, shows who accepted the invitation, and plays a call's recording right in the app.

## New

- **Open in Google Calendar.** A meeting card has an "Open in Google Calendar" button next to the join buttons. It opens the event on its own page in the calendar. This needs memo 0.138.0 or newer.
- **Invitation answers.** Each participant on the card shows whether they accepted, declined or have not answered. A declined answer is shown in red. Your own answer is in Details.
- **How a meeting repeats.** Details says how a repeating meeting repeats, for example every week on weekdays, and names the conference.
- **The event's Drive files.** Documents on the card now also list the files attached to the calendar event.
- **Listen to a call's recording.** A call with a recording on this computer has a Listen button on its card. A large file or an unusual format opens in your own player. A recording kept only on the phone system says so.
- **Meeting links.** A link to a meeting or a call opens its card on the Meetings page.

## All changes

- A meeting link opens the call, and its recording plays here (#397)
- The meeting card opens the event in its calendar, and shows answers, how it repeats and its Drive files (#395)
