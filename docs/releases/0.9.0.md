WireCat 0.9.0 makes your tools take much less disk space. They now share one copy of the files they have in common. It also adds Slack, new Contacts tabs and one-click updates through apt on Linux.

## New

- **Tools share their files.** WireCat now installs its tools with its own copy of pnpm. Files that several tools use are kept once on the disk. Six tools took about 1.9 GB before and take about 600 MB now. You do not need to do anything, and you do not need to install pnpm.
- **Tools you installed with npm move by themselves.** On a tool's next update, WireCat installs the new version its own way and checks that it works. It then moves the tool's background service to the new copy, and only after that removes the npm copy. If a step fails, the old copy stays and keeps working. A copy WireCat cannot remove is listed on the tool's row, with the command to remove it.
- **Slack.** Add Slack on the Tools page and sign in inside the window. Your agent can then read and search your channels, direct messages and threads.
- **Contacts has three tabs:** People, Bots and Organizations. Each sender is on one of them.
- **One-click updates through apt on Linux.** A copy installed from apt.wirecat.dev installs the update when you click Update. The system asks for your password in its own window when it needs to.

## Improved

- An official tool's commands now come from the tool itself, so the app and the tool always agree.
- The Bitrix24 sign-in steps list every permission the tool needs, so calls and documents sync too.
- The Digests page says how many parts of a digest were left out because of a format error.

## Fixed

- A digest the agent wrote with a format error is no longer called an old digest. The page names the field that was wrong.
- Telegram, MAX, WhatsApp and Slack list their accounts again after the tools' latest releases.
- The check screen warns again when MAX has no bot token.
- The Yandex Disk sign-in names the right Yandex site for the OAuth app.
