WireCat 0.6.4 adds a Meetings page and a Documents page, and chat agents now connect with full rights unless you choose read only. The Meetings and Documents pages need Memo 0.133.0 or newer: WireCat offers the update.

## New

- **Meetings.** A new page shows your meetings, calls and calendar events as one list. Choose Week, Month or Table. Open a meeting to see its join links, summary, participants, documents and transcript, and find words in the transcript.
- **Documents.** A new page shows every document Memo keeps: Drive files, notes folders, added files and imports. A folder tree per account sits on the left and a table on the right. Open a document to read its text, its tags, the people it names and its meetings.
- **Chat agents and read only.** Hermes and OpenClaw now connect with the rights your tools already give, so sync works for them. If you want them to only read, turn on "Connect chat agents read only" in the connect window or in Settings. Anyone who can message the agent's bot can ask it to send, change or delete, so turn it on if strangers can reach the bot.

## Improved

- **Times for today.** Times from today show hours and minutes, without seconds, except in the run log.
- **Telemost notes in Russian.** The mail rules view calls Telemost's notes «Конспекты встреч», as Yandex does.

## All changes

- Chat agents connect with full rights by default, read only behind a switch (#382)
- Telemost's notes are called «Конспекты встреч» in the mail rules view (#379)
- Today's times without seconds outside the run log (#378)
- A Meetings page with Week, Month and Table views (#377)
- A Documents page over the documents Memo keeps (#376)
