WireCat 0.7.5 shows how fresh your WhatsApp data is, signs in to WordPress, and opens mail rules in a searchable dialog.

## New

- **How fresh WhatsApp data is.** A WhatsApp account says "Live" while its background service is connected, and "Fresh as of 9:05 AM" when it is not. It needs whatsapp-cli 0.46.0 or later.
- **WordPress in the catalog.** It signs in with the site, a user name and an application password, in a form, with no terminal. The password goes to the tool on its input, never as an argument.
- **Mail rules in a dialog**, with search and pages. The account row shows the number of rules.
- **Inbox shows the sender** of each thread, and a finished "Sort now" names the model and its cost.

## Improved

- **App Center shows when WireCat was released** for a copy installed from apt.wirecat.dev, instead of "Unknown".
- amoCRM, Attio and HubSpot link their pages on wirecat.dev.
- The question before mail goes to a model outside this computer is in your language.
