WireCat 0.7.0 adds Notion, groups the Tools page, with a new group for additional WireCat tools, always shows "Add your own tool", updates every tool with one click, opens a deal's company in its card, shows who called whom, and plays beeline calls in the app.

## New

- **Notion.** Notion is a built-in tool. Sign in with a Notion token in the window. WireCat copies the pages and databases that the token can see to this computer, so you can search them and your agent can read them.
- **Tool groups.** The Tools page lists built-in tools, additional WireCat tools, community tools and tools WireCat has not checked, each under its own heading. An empty group is not shown.
- **Additional WireCat tools.** A WireCat tool that is not built into this version, such as beeline, has its own group and the badge "Verified by WireCat". Its review says WireCat makes it, without the warning about unknown authors.
- **Add your own tool is always there.** The button is under the tool list. If "Allow tools WireCat has not checked" is off, the dialog shows that setting's warning first, and "Allow and continue" turns it on.
- **Update all.** Next to "Install all", "Update all" updates every installed tool that has a newer version, one after another. Your own tools stay out, because each update goes through its review.
- **A deal's company card.** The company of a deal or a lead opens in its card, with its people, open deals and leads, and how many calls its people had. A person's calls load when you open that person.
- **Beeline calls play in WireCat.** A tool you added yourself can say how its calls open, so beeline's calls open from the client card and the Meetings page. beeline 0.3.0 needs WireCat 0.7.0, so update WireCat first.
- **A tool's own sign-in steps.** A tool that describes its sign-in, such as Asterisk from version 0.7.0, gets its own steps in the sign-in window.

## Improved

- **Call details.** A call row shows whether the call was incoming or outgoing, the manager and the client's number.
- **A missing profile.** When a command needs a profile, the window lists your profiles, or says that signing in makes the first one, instead of a command line to type.

## Fixed

- **No license to accept.** The macOS disk image and the Windows installer no longer ask you to accept the license. The license still comes with the app.
