WireCat 0.6.0 adds three new pages, Contacts, Clients and Digests, and a reworked Memory page. It also brings one setup wizard for every tool, full account removal, your own tools, and pages that open much faster.

Memory, Contacts, Clients, Digests and Tasks are marked beta. You see a β beside their names.

## New

- **Contacts.** A new page shows who is who across Telegram, MAX, WhatsApp, Email, meetings and Bitrix24 CRM. Tabs sort them into People, Companies, Newsletters and notifications, Strangers and Unsorted, each with its count. The table shows phones, emails, message counts and tasks, 50 rows a page. Open a person to see their accounts, move accounts to a new person, or undo a join. People who may be the same person show as one card per group. The card shows the shared value, for example the same phone number. Open a match to compare the people side by side: where each one comes from, their contacts, company, deals, tasks and latest messages. Join two or all of them. Agents can also suggest that a person is really two people, and you answer those suggestions here.
- **Clients.** A new page shows your CRM deals and leads, as a table or as a board by stage. Change a deal's stage with a drag on the board, in the table, or in the deal's card. The change goes to Bitrix24 too. "Map stages" puts each CRM stage under one of WireCat's stages. Suggestions wait in an Unmapped column, where you accept them with one click. The sync copies only the deals of the CRM users you choose, not the whole portal. Bitrix24 asks you to choose them once, in its command line tool, before the first sync.
- **Digests.** A new page lists your digests in a table: name, how often each runs, what it reads, where it sends, and the last and next run. Create a digest, run it twice a day or on weekdays, and set a cost limit. "Get now" makes one at once. Edit what it collects and how it looks for Telegram, Email and plain text, with a preview. Build past days to read them in the app. A notification tells you when a new digest is ready.
- **Memory explains itself.** A band says how it works: an agent suggests, you review, agents use it. Each row reads as a sentence, with a status word: "Waiting for you", "In use" or "Rejected". Open a row to see its source, what agents will know, and what Approve and Reject change. You can edit a row. Relationships between people and companies show with their standing, and you confirm or reject them in Review.
- **Add to memory.** Give WireCat pasted text, a link, a file or a picture. It keeps it as a searchable document. A picture is searchable by its contents only when an AI model reads it, otherwise it is kept by its name. Then ask for suggested facts and keep the ones you tick. When the source of a memory changes or goes away, the row says so and shows the source before and after.
- **AI model in Settings.** Choose one AI provider and model (Anthropic, OpenAI or a compatible one, or Ollama), and set one key. Check tells you in plain words if it works. With "Read pictures with this model" on, Add to memory uses it to read pictures, and "Reading for facts" can use it too. The key is never shown, and the page says where it is kept.
- **Better search, when you allow it.** Two settings, off by default, each with a consent step that shows what leaves your computer, where it goes and what it costs. "Rewrite records for better search" adds other words to your notes, documents and emails, so a question in other words finds them. "Search harder" lets a hosted model reorder the top results of one search you start with its button. You can also choose the local search model, and the best one is marked. A new card on Memo's details page helps you set up your notes: choose your notes folders, read them, and turn on search by meaning.
- **One setup wizard for every tool.** Every sign-in says what to have at hand, shows its steps on the left, and has a visible Back. A failure says what happened, the likely cause and one fix. New ways in: MAX by token, WhatsApp by phone number and code, Zoom with a step-by-step checklist. Gmail signs in with Google first, with no Google Cloud setup of your own. The app password is still there under other ways, with guided steps. Every link has a copy button. The Done step has a card for each tool with next steps: add another account, start a sync, keep it running in the background, connect an AI app and more.
- **Bitrix24 signs in inside WireCat.** Type your portal's address, then follow the steps to make an incoming webhook there: a button opens your portal's Developer resources, and each right to tick is listed with its code and a copy button. Paste the webhook's address and WireCat checks it with the portal, with no terminal. A missing right, a webhook of another portal, a wrong address, or a webhook another profile already uses each says what to do. The address stays on your computer, in the system keyring.
- **Rename and sign out any account.** The account menu of every tool now has Rename, Sign in again, Sign out (Zoom has no sign-out yet) and Remove account. A name you give an account shows on the Tools page, in Settings and in Search, with its profile beside it. Your agent still uses the profile.
- **Remove an account fully.** The account menu has Remove account. Choose to keep or delete the account's data, then see what goes and what stays before you confirm. This works for MAX, WhatsApp, Email, Drive, Zoom and Google Calendar.
- **Add your own tool.** Turn on "Allow tools WireCat has not checked" in Settings, then type a tool's npm name to add it. Before it installs, WireCat shows a review: a "Not verified" badge, what the tool says it does in plain words, links, things to check, and an optional AI check. No restart is needed after you add or remove one.
- **wirecat:// links.** A link to a chat, a message, a task or a digest now opens it in the running app. A chat or message opens in a read-only conversation screen, with the linked message marked.
- **Check for updates from the Help menu.** It says if you are up to date, offers the update, or says why the check failed.
- **Copy the id of what you see.** A small copy button sits beside a task, its source, a memory row and its sources, a finding, a search hit, a person and a deal with its people and company. It copies the id your agent takes, for example `task:HRB-14` or `fact:21`, even where the id is not printed.
- **A deal shows its client's tasks.** Open a deal or a lead to see the tasks of its contact people, with their status and due date. A task opens in its own window.
- **Open in more agents.** "Open in" now offers every agent WireCat finds on this computer, not only Claude Code, Codex and Gemini CLI. On Linux and macOS the agent starts with the request itself, so you see what was asked. On Windows it still starts with the path of the saved request.

## Improved

- **Import records from a file.** In Storage, download a template for a record type, fill it in as CSV or JSON, check it, and import it. The check lists each refused record with its line and column.
- **Pages open much faster.** WireCat keeps Memo running in the background and sends a page's reads to it at the same time. A page you opened in the last minute shows at once. Tasks and Contacts no longer load every contact to show names.
- **Each page says which tool it needs.** When a tool is missing, too old or signed out, the page says so in the same words everywhere, with one button that goes to the fix.
- **Without Memo, Settings and links still say what is missing.** AI model, Reading for facts and a chat opened from a link now say Memo is missing or too old in the same words as other pages, with the Install or Update button. Before, AI model and Reading for facts were hidden. The tool's own error text is under Details on Clients and conversations. The Tasks made tile in Analytics no longer waits forever without Memo.
- **A Memo that hangs at start costs less.** WireCat waits longer between tries each time, up to about half an hour, so pages do not wait 10 seconds for it every 2 minutes.
- **Buttons stay clickable.** A button no longer turns grey when a value is missing. Click it, and the field says what to fix.
- **Tasks is calmer.** Cards are short, columns page their cards, and a filter picks the source. Tasks found in your chats now show, with a readable title. Close a found task from its status, and save it without errors. New tasks open in a dialog, and you find an assignee by name.
- **Analytics says why a number is missing**, and offers the fix: install, update, sign in or try again. Counts for 30 and 90 days now show. A muted chat still counts as a chat, but its messages do not count.
- **One account row everywhere.** Each account shows its status, last sync and a Sync button. A menu holds Check, Choose folders and Sign in again. Drive folders can be changed after sign-in. "Add a bot" is now in the sign-in plan, on the Tools row and in the first setup.
- **Search** in Telegram, MAX and WhatsApp gives up to 30 seconds to a search. A search that runs out of time says the query is fine and offers Try again.
- **Store damage report.** When a tool's message store is damaged, its check shows what happened and the steps to fix it, with a Run button for the safe ones. Logs can filter by kind and table.
- **Community tools.** When the community list runs out, a notice at the top says so. Installed community tools keep working, but you cannot install or update them until the list is fresh again.
- **Service logos.** Tools, Contacts, match cards, search results, Analytics filters and Settings show each service's logo, with its name in a tooltip.
- **Clients notices fold into one line of chips.** Open a chip to read its notice. The board starts higher on the page. "Accept all" in the stage chip confirms every suggested stage at once.
- **Shared mailboxes in Contacts.** Everyone on one shared address is one card, marked as a shared mailbox. "Company mailbox" gives the address to a company, so WireCat stops suggesting a join. Matches now come surest first.
- **Links in Memory.** A link's role comes from a list in your language, and you can add a role. A link to a document shows its title, folder, change date and an Open button. Memory lists only what you can decide there, with your own links first.
- **Settings for the AI model.** The reader is a plain dropdown, with "No model (use your agent)". Ollama and OpenRouter list their models. For Anthropic and OpenAI you type the model name. The note about pictures says exactly what happens.
- **Search and Analytics.** Search shows one short result line. Analytics counts tasks and meeting hours correctly. The chart of when you talk can show everyone or only you, and one messenger at a time.
- **Logs filters** stand in columns, each count beside its label.
- **Match cards say what matched.** A card lists the name, company, domain or phone that matched, with the value, in your language.
- **A person's card in Contacts says more.** Each account shows the profiles of yours it was seen in, a bot mark, and its own Unlink button that asks first. The tasks assigned to the person are listed, and each opens in Tasks. Moving accounts to a new person shows each account's service icon.
- **Contacts table rows keep one height.** A person with a phone and an email shows the first and "+1". A person with only a WhatsApp or MAX account shows that service instead of a dash. Bots are marked.
- **Check links in plain words.** Each person is a card, the name opens it, and each account shows its service. "These belong together" settles a doubt in one click.
- **Ended sign-ins.** Every tool says it the same way, with one Reconnect button. The tool's own text is under Details.
- **Bots have their own controls.** Status, reconnect and remove with a confirmation. On wide windows bots stand in a column beside the accounts.
- **Errors in plain words.** About 70 messages keep the technical text under Details. A failed read in Memory offers Read again, and the update when the tool is too old.
- **Sync on a schedule** is a normal button, and reads "Change schedule" when it is on. Telegram, MAX and Email can now sync every 2, 4, 8 or 24 hours too.
- **Every page looks the same.** Titles, frames, notices and section headings are now one size and one style on every page, Tasks and Clients included.
- **The empty search bar** lists filter keys, the ones you use most first, and a written query matches part of a name (`from:*Мила*`).
- **One Open in button for your agents.** Guides, the Fix panel and a community tool's check have one button with Claude Code, Codex and Gemini CLI in its menu. It opens the agent you used last, and an agent that is not installed shows how to install it.
- **Guides say what memo reads.** A guide that asks memo lists every source WireCat syncs, not only memory.
- **Contacts accounts open where they live.** In a person's card, each account links to its page: a Bitrix24 contact or user on your portal, Telegram by username, WhatsApp by number. An email address opens your mail program. A copy button beside each copies the address, number or handle.
- **Company mailboxes name who writes from them.** After "Company mailbox", tick the people who write from the address. They stay separate people and are saved as the company's contacts.
- **Test data is hidden for you.** Memo hides accounts at test addresses, such as example.com or .test, everywhere. Check the links shows one line, "1 test account is hidden · Show", and each hidden person has "It is a real contact". A card memo is unsure about asks "It is test data" or "It is a real contact", and your answer holds on every computer. "Separate test accounts" still moves test accounts off a real person.
- **Every phone and address in a person's card.** Each account lists all the phones and addresses its source gave, a Bitrix24 contact's several included. A phone opens your phone program, an address your mail program, and each has a copy button.
- **Memory findings, one by one.** An agent's findings fill the whole width of their row. Approve or reject each one, or all at once, and Save says how many it creates and how many it rejects. The labels say what a fact and a task are. Each source shows as a name you can read, such as the mailbox or the chat, and opens or copies from there. Who recorded the findings and when is under More details. After you save, edit the fact or task a finding became. Approve all on this page says what it approves, and its result reads as a sentence.
- **Digests are easier to set up.** Pick sources from a list you can search. Each field of "Send to" has a label and a short hint. Cost limits have − and + buttons. "What to collect" starts from a template for a daily, weekly or own digest, which you edit.
- **Clients says what its sync does.** The line above the search names whose deals are downloaded, when the last sync ran, and has a Sync button.
- **Approve and Reject look like buttons.** Each finding has two clear buttons, and the chosen one is marked. "Approve all" and "Reject all" sit above the list. An opened batch closes from the same button that opened it.

## Fixed

- **Memory decisions work again.** On the Memory page, approve, reject, correct and forget work again.
- **No Drive document owners to review.** Memory no longer lists them, and never shows raw data as a reason.
- **Links and agents on NixOS.** Sign-in pages open in your browser, and agents installed with Nix are found. When a link cannot open, a dialog shows the address to copy. Right-click any web link to copy it.
- **One digest notification.** While WireCat runs, a new digest shows one notification, not two.
- **Tools page layout.** The page no longer cuts off text in narrow windows or in Russian, and the "Add a bot" tooltip shows in full. Each tool keeps its buttons on one line with its name, and says in a few words what it does ("Chats and channels · search · replies"); the sync line shows only while a sync runs or just ended.
- **The window opens where it was**, at a size that fits your screen.
- **Boot screen.** It no longer changes size while it loads.
- **Undo a join.** The dialog and the person's list of joins no longer say "No name yet". They name the person when WireCat still knows the name, and the dialog lists each account that goes back.
- **Digests from one template read apart.** A second weekly digest is called "Weekly digest 2". A title you type stays as you typed it.
- **A shared phone number that no account shows** now says "number hidden: accounts keep only its fingerprint" on a match card, instead of a bare "same".
- **Names.** A person with no name of their own shows the account's name, not the username. Tasks now names people the same way Contacts does.
- **Check the links no longer doubts one person's address.** A short form of a name, such as Ярик for Ярослав, or an account named after its own mailbox, no longer shows as a shared address.
- **Check the links keeps your place.** Hide stays where it was, the panel opens and closes smoothly, and a new page of people keeps the panel in view. Each card has an Open button, and a name with no letters, such as "—", is replaced by the person's account name. An account named "." shows as having no name.
- **Dragged cards** land from where you let them go. Digest buttons line up.
- **Moving accounts off a person** now says which person they leave, so WireCat cannot take an account from someone else.
- **Saving a task is steady.** The dialog no longer flashes, and the button stays and says Saved. A close or a link warns before it drops what you typed. If a task changed under you, your text stays, and Save or Cancel decides.
- **No false rate limit.** A pause is now described as a pause. "Stop starting at login" shows only for a service that does start at login.
- **Tasks from your messages.** The task dialog shows the message Memo found the task in: the chat, who wrote it, when, and its words, with Open in the chat and what to do next. The project row says No project, not the messenger's name. The dialog says plainly why such a task has only three statuses and no priority, type, agent or project. A task you assigned shows its assignee when you open it. A search or filter says that it leaves tasks from messages out, because Memo filters only project tasks.
- **Tasks from messages on the board.** Their cards move again: drop one on Done or Dismissed to close it. A drop on another status says why it cannot go there. In the list and the dialog, priority, type, agent and project of such a task open a note that says why they do not change and what to do, and the status list shows the project-only statuses under their own heading. The line under the title says "From messages: Telegram", not a name that reads like a project.
- **Menus in the task list are no longer cut off.** A status or other menu in a row opens above the rows below it.
- **Info tips in dialogs.** The ⓘ beside a field in a dialog, such as AI agent in a task, shows its text again on hover and click.
- **Mail findings say what was found.** An agent's findings from your mail now list each one with its kind (task, invoice, payment, order, new contact and more), why the agent thinks so, and the email it read, which you can open. Approve keeps only the ones you ticked; before, it kept all of them unseen.
- **Meeting hours keep last week.** When Zoom has not synced this week, the card still shows last week's hours and says to sync Zoom. The Zoom logo is sharp, with rounded corners.
- **Scrollbars and the logo.** A scrollbar shows when the pointer is in its column, and the sidebar logo keeps its gap in short, wide windows.
- **Full names in Contacts.** A name of one or two letters, such as "Вр", gives way to the full name an account knows. A match card no longer repeats a name that differs only in case, and a card of three or more people says "Merge all 3".
- **Contacts loads without jumps.** The page waits until it knows what goes above the table, then appears once, with matches, Check the links and the table where they stay.
- **Digests rows keep their height.** Turning a digest on or off no longer makes its row taller or shorter. The next run fades in.
- **Folded groups no longer use up Memory's pages.** A folded group takes one line of a page, its header, so the other groups' rows fill the page. Before, a folded group of 86 records left four pages that looked empty.
- **Memory keeps your place.** A new page of the list scrolls up to its first row, and nothing below it jumps. Titles show a hand pointer, with the arrow right after the text. A long email reference no longer runs off the edge. When memory cannot be read, the page offers Try again, and offers the update only when Memo is too old.

## Known issues

- **Telegram and Bitrix24 accounts are only signed out** when you remove them. Full removal comes in a later version of WireCat.
