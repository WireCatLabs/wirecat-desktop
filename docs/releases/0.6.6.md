WireCat 0.6.6 connects your Obsidian vault to memo, makes the Meetings week easier to read, and makes the app calmer: tabs switch smoothly, links open the right place, and errors say what really happened.

## New

- **Connect an Obsidian vault.** Next to "Choose a folder", the notes card now has "Connect an Obsidian vault". It also lists the vaults Obsidian already knows, with a Connect button for each. A plain folder is read as Markdown, so a `#word` in it is no longer a tag. This needs memo 0.134.0. The card asks you to update memo when it is older.
- **Digests have their own tab.** Tools now has a Digests tab with every digest, its schedule and its days. The Agents tab shows only agents.
- **Countries of your phone lines for Asterisk.** The Asterisk sign-in form asks which countries your phone lines are in. Then WireCat can link a caller without a country code to the right contact.
- **Search meetings.** The Meetings page has a search field with suggestions, as on the other pages.

## Improved

- **Overlapping meetings.** Meetings at the same time now sit side by side or over each other, as in Google Calendar. You see "+N" only when there is no room.
- **A fuller meeting card.** The card shows the place, the calendar, the join links, the notes and the recording when the meeting has them, and a link to open the meeting where it came from.
- **A smaller account picker on Documents.**
- **Smooth tab switches.** Switching tabs no longer flashes an empty page or a loading line.
- **Links open their target.** A link such as "Update Memo" on Documents now opens Tools at Memo's row and highlights it. The same goes for links to settings and to digests.
- **No jump after an install.** After you press Done, the tool's settings open smoothly under its row.
- **The pointer shows over tabs.**
- **A clearer card in Ubuntu App Center.** The Linux packages now carry the app's description and its license.

## Fixed

- **A failed digest is easy to find.** A digest whose last run failed comes first on the Digests tab. Its day says the run failed. A notification about it opens that digest.
- **Errors say what holds a tool.** If a sync, a sign-in or another update is running, an install or update now says which one, instead of a general message. During a sync you can stop it from the same panel.
